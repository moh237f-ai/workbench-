## Bugs found in review → fixed by me
1. **CRITICAL — `.rd-link-layer` had no `pointer-events: none`**: the layer div spans the whole page (inset 0) at z-index 5 ABOVE the ink canvas (z-index 2), so on ANY page with link annotations it intercepted every pointerdown → pen/highlighter/eraser were dead on exactly the pages that have TOC/cross-ref links, and the full page surface (not just zones) swallowed clicks. Fix: `pointer-events: none` on the container; zones re-enable with their existing inline `pointer-events: auto` (select mode only). This is the standard pattern (children can opt back in inside a pointer-events:none parent) and matches the brief's "drawing tools must not be blocked".
2. **Hardening — `ann.url` was trusted as-is**: pdf.js marks its `url` field "safe", but it's still untrusted PDF input; a `javascript:`-style value would flow straight to window.open. Now BOTH `url` and `unsafeUrl` go through `sanitizeExternalUrl` (absolute http/https/mailto only, URL-parsed).
